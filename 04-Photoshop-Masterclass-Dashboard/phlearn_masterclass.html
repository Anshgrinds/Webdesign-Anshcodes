Rule 01
Never Delete Pixels
Black conceals, white reveals. Always attach Layer Masks instead of using the destructive Eraser tool.
Rule 02
Smart Objects First
Convert layers to Smart Objects before transforms or filters to retain full raw resolution and editable Smart Filters.
Rule 03
Frequency Split
Never blur skin. Split High Frequency (Pores/Texture) from Low Frequency (Skin Tone/Color) using Apply Image math.
Rule 04
5 Pillars Compositing
Match Perspective, Scale, Luminance range, Color temperature, and Uniform film grain across every composite.

Technical Retouching Mathematics
Frequency Separation Math (8-Bit vs 16-Bit)
// 8-BIT IMAGES:
1. Low Frequency: Filter > Blur > Gaussian Blur (Radius: ~3.0px)
2. High Frequency: Image > Apply Image:
   - Layer: Low Frequency
   - Blending: Subtract
   - Scale: 2
   - Offset: 128
3. High Frequency Blend Mode: Linear Light

// 16-BIT IMAGES:
1. Low Frequency: Gaussian Blur (Radius: ~3.0px)
2. High Frequency: Image > Apply Image:
   - Layer: Low Frequency
   - Invert: CHECKED
   - Blending: Add
   - Scale: 2
   - Offset: 0
3. High Frequency Blend Mode: Linear Light
