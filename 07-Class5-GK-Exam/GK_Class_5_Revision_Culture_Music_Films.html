PART 2: SECTION C REVISION — Traditional Music & Nepali Films Cheat Sheet & Milestones
🪕 Traditional Musical Instruments

• Madal: The most popular two-headed wooden hand drum wrapped in leather; played in folk songs & dances across Nepal.

• Sarangi: 4-string bowed instrument played exclusively by the Gandharva (Gaine) community to recite news & folk tales.

• Bansuri & Murali: Transverse flutes carved from high-quality bamboo; heart of traditional folk tunes.

• Damphu: Round frame drum held in hand and played during Tamang Selo ceremonies.

• Panche Baja: Traditional ensemble of 5 instruments: Damaha, Tyanko, Dholak, Sahanai, and Khyali/Jhyali.

• Dhime: Large, thunderous wooden drum played during Newari Jatras.

🎬 Golden Milestones of Nepali Cinema & Icons

• Satya Harishchandra (1951): First-ever Nepali language film (produced in Kolkata, India by D.B. Pariyar).

• Aama (Mother - 1964): First feature film produced inside Nepal by the Government Information Dept (Dir: Hira Singh Khatri).

• Maitighar (1966): First film produced under a private studio banner (Sumonanjali Films).

• Kumari (1982): The first full-length Nepali color film.

• Narayan Gopal: Revering title 'Swor Samrat' (King of Voice).

• Tara Devi: 'Swor Kinnari' (recorded over 4,000 evergreen songs).

• Aruna Lama: 'Nightingale of the Hills'.
