SECTION B: SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS [20 Marks]
Q1. Write a short note on Paropakar Sanstha and its contribution to Nepali society.
Model Answer:
Paropakar Sanstha is one of the oldest social organizations in Nepal, founded by Dayaram Bhakta Mathema in 2004 B.S.
Key contributions:
1. Established orphanages and provided shelter, food, and education for helpless children.
2. Distributed free medical assistance and ran ambulance services during cholera and epidemics.
Q2. Why is "Active Listening" considered the most important skill in managing conflicts?
Model Answer:
Most conflicts happen because people shout instead of understanding. Active listening helps because:
1. It calms down high emotions when the other person feels their feelings are heard and respected.
2. It reveals the true reason behind the argument so friends can find a fair, win-win compromise.
Q3. What is Child Labor? Why should children not be employed in heavy work?
Model Answer:
Child labor refers to the exploitation of young children in work that deprives them of their childhood, regular school attendance, and mental/physical well-being.
Children should not be employed because heavy physical labor stunts their growth, causes permanent health damage, and robs them of their fundamental right to education.
Q4. How does the District Coordination Committee monitor development projects in a district?
Model Answer:
The DCC conducts field inspections and reviews:
1. It checks whether roads, suspension bridges, and drinking water pipes are being built properly with quality materials.
2. It verifies that development funds are used transparently and projects are completed within deadlines.
Q5. Why was Kathmandu Valley inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List? Name any two monument zones.
Model Answer:
Kathmandu Valley was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List (1979) because of its exceptional architectural masterworks, historic palace squares, and unique fusion of Hindu and Buddhist cultures.
Two monument zones: 1. Swayambhunath Stupa • 2. Patan Durbar Square (or Bhaktapur Durbar Square).
Q6. State three duties of a student towards their nation (country).
Model Answer:
1. Respect national symbols: Stand with pride for the National Anthem and honor the National Flag.
2. Protect public property and avoid littering national parks or historic monuments.
3. Study well and cultivate good moral values to become a productive, honest citizen in the future.
SECTION C: CREATIVE & VALUE-BASED QUESTIONS [15 Marks]
Q1. Group Work Conflict Scenario:

"In a Class 5 Social Studies group assignment, two group members, Aryan and Sabina, started arguing. Aryan wanted to make a model of Mount Everest, while Sabina insisted on making a model of Pashupatinath Temple. Neither was ready to listen."

As the Group Leader, how would you resolve this conflict fairly?
1. Calm them down: Remind both that both ideas are excellent examples of Nepal's heritage (one Natural, one Cultural).
2. Propose a Win-Win Solution: Split the chart/diorama board into two sections: display the natural peak of Mount Everest on the left and the cultural temple of Pashupatinath on the right.
3. Divide the tasks: Have Aryan lead the Everest model and Sabina lead the temple model so both talents are celebrated.
