SECTION B: SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS [20 Marks]
Q1. Mention three important contributions of a local Youth Club or Mother’s Group (Aama Samuha).
Model Answer:
1. Social Awareness: They run campaigns against bad habits like gambling, smoking, and caste discrimination.
2. Community Cleanliness: They organize tree plantation programs and clean community water springs and drainage.
3. Cultural Preservation: They organize traditional sports, Deusi-Bhailo, and festivals to keep folk traditions alive.
Q2. What is "Empathy"? Give one example of how empathy prevents arguments in the classroom.
Model Answer:
Empathy means understanding and sharing another person's feelings by placing yourself in their shoes.
Example: If a classmate accidentally bumps into you and drops their water bottle, instead of getting angry, empathy makes you realize: "They must be nervous and embarrassed right now." You help them pick it up, preventing a fight.
Q3. What are the four major pillars of Child Rights recognized by the United Nations?
Model Answer:
1. Right to Survival: Basic food, clean water, shelter, and medical care.
2. Right to Development: Education, access to books, recreation, and sports.
3. Right to Protection: Safety from child labor, neglect, violence, and abuse.
4. Right to Participation: Freedom to express thoughts and participate in school/family discussions.
Q4. List three duties children have towards their teachers and school.
Model Answer:
1. Greet teachers respectfully and listen attentively during class.
2. Complete classwork and homework honestly and on time.
3. Take care of school property like computer labs, library books, and sports equipment.
Q5. Why is the District Coordination Committee called an umbrella coordinating body?
Model Answer:
Because it does not rule over local municipalities with force; instead, it brings all rural municipalities and municipalities in the district under one common umbrella. It ensures that neighboring areas cooperate fairly on roads, rivers, and health centers without disputes.
Q6. State three reasons why tourists from all over the world travel to Nepal to visit heritage sites.
Model Answer:
1. To see the world's highest peak, Mount Everest (Sagarmatha), and rare wildlife like the one-horned rhino.
2. To experience sacred pilgrimage sites like Lumbini (Buddha's birthplace) and Pashupatinath.
3. To admire unique ancient wooden and stone pagoda architecture found in the historic Durbar Squares of Kathmandu Valley.
SECTION C: CREATIVE & VALUE-BASED QUESTIONS [15 Marks]
Q1. School Bus Bullying Scenario:

"Your friend Bikash tells you that an older student constantly pushes him and calls him silly nicknames on the school bus every day. Bikash feels scared and wants to skip school."

What advice and action steps should you take to help your friend?
1. Reassure Bikash: Tell him it is NOT his fault and he should never feel ashamed or hide it.
2. Speak firmly: Advise him to tell the older student in a calm, clear voice: "Please stop, I don't like this."
3. Report immediately: Accompany Bikash to the class teacher or bus in-charge and report the bullying safely so adults can take immediate corrective action.
Q2. Student Action Plan for Local Monument Conservation:

Imagine there is an ancient stone water spout (*Dhunge Dhara*) near your house that has dried up and is full of weeds and trash. How can you and your neighborhood friends help restore it?

Model Answer:
1. Form a small "Junior Eco-Heritage Team" with friends and clean up the surrounding garbage and dry leaves.
2. Request the local Ward Office and elders to inspect the underground water pipeline and repair leaks.
3. Plant small flower beds nearby to protect the perimeter and keep stray animals away.
4. Place a wooden signboard saying: "Our Historic Stone Spout — Please Keep Clean."
Q3. My Civic Pledge (Fill in the blanks to complete your pledge):
I, as a responsible student of Class 5, proudly pledge:
1. I will always solve disagreements with my friends through calm dialogue and patience.
2. I will balance my rights by fulfilling my duties to keep my school and home clean and disciplined.
