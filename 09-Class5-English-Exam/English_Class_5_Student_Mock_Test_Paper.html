SECTION A: READING COMPREHENSION 8 Marks
Q1. Read the following extract adapted from Ruskin Bond's story and answer the questions below: [8 Marks]
When Grandfather visited the Lucknow Zoo six months later, he went straight to the large cage where Timothy had been lodged. Inside the cage was a magnificent full-grown tiger, pacing back and forth. Grandfather approached the iron bars without fear. He put both his arms through the railings, stroked the tiger’s forehead, and tickled its ears. Whenever the tiger gave a low growl, Grandfather playfully smacked him across the mouth—which was his old way of keeping Timothy quiet.

The tiger licked Grandfather’s hands with relish and purred loudly, springing back only when a noisy leopard in the adjacent cage snarled. A crowd began to gather, watching this daring old gentleman with wide eyes. Presently, a zoo keeper who remembered Grandfather came rushing over in utter panic. "What are you doing, sir?" shouted the keeper breathlessly. "Step back! That is not your tiger! Your Timothy died two months ago of pneumonia. This beast was trapped in the hills only last month, and he is fierce and dangerous!"
