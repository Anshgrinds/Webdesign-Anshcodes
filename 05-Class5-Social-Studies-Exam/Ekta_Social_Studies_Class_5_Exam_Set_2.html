II. State whether the following statements are 'True' or 'False':
  1. Joining the Scouts helps students develop leadership and self-reliance.
  2. Suppressing your anger and refusing to talk to anyone is the best way to handle conflict.
  3. Children have the right to be protected from dangerous labor and physical punishment.
  4. The District Coordination Committee has the power to pass national laws for the whole country.
  5. Natural heritage includes mountains, rivers, waterfalls, and national parks.
Answers:
1. True
2. False (Open dialogue and communication solve conflict, not silent grudges)
3. True
4. False (Only the Federal Parliament passes national laws; the DCC coordinates and monitors local development)
5. True

SECTION B: SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS [20 Marks]
Q1. What is the Nepal Scout? Write any two benefits a student gains by joining the Scouts.
Model Answer:
Nepal Scouts is a volunteer social organization that develops character, physical fitness, and civic responsibility in young students.
Two benefits:
1. Students learn essential life skills such as first-aid, knot tying, and disaster rescue.
2. It builds self-confidence, team spirit, and a habit of helping elderly and vulnerable citizens.
Q2. What role does a teacher or class monitor play as a mediator when classmates quarrel?
Model Answer:
A mediator acts as a fair, unbiased guide. They:
1. Make both students sit calmly and express their points without interrupting or insulting each other.
2. Identify the root misunderstanding and encourage both friends to apologize and shake hands.
Q3. Write four basic rights that every child is entitled to according to the UN Child Rights Convention.
Model Answer:
1. Right to Identity: Having a name, birth registration, and nationality.
2. Right to Education: Going to school and learning in a safe environment.
3. Right to Health: Getting nutritious food, safe drinking water, and medical care.
4. Right to Play & Rest: Having leisure time for recreation and sports.
Q4. Mention three duties of a student towards public property and public facilities.
Model Answer:
1. Do not break or scribble on public bus seats, street benches, or road signs.
2. Turn off community water taps after use to prevent wastage of drinking water.
3. Never pluck decorative flowers or damage trees planted along public roadsides.
Q5. Explain the tenure (term of office) and minimum female representation required in the DCC.
Model Answer:
• Tenure: Members of the District Coordination Committee serve for a term of 5 years.
• Representation: Out of the maximum 9 members, at least three women and at least one person from the Dalit or minority community must be included to ensure fairness and inclusion.
Q6. Distinguish between Tangible (Physical) and Intangible Cultural Heritage with examples.
Model Answer:
• Tangible Cultural Heritage: Physical monuments, temples, palace courtyards, and statues that can be seen and touched (e.g., Patan Durbar Square, Boudhanath Stupa).
• Intangible Cultural Heritage: Living traditions, folk dances, musical instruments, languages, and festivals passed through generations (e.g., Dashain, Tihar, Newari Lakhey dance, Madal songs).
SECTION C: CREATIVE & VALUE-BASED QUESTIONS [15 Marks]
Q1. Situation-based Conflict Resolution:

"Rita borrowed Suman's colored pencils and accidentally broke the tip of the red pencil. Suman got furious, shouted at Rita, and snatched Rita's drawing notebook."

a) Was Suman's reaction right? Why or why not?
No, Suman's reaction was wrong. Accidents happen, and reacting with anger and retaliation damages friendships.

b) What should Rita have said, and how should Suman have reacted?
Rita should have immediately said: "I'm so sorry, Suman! It was an accident. Let me sharpen it for you right now." Suman should have accepted the apology politely instead of snatching her notebook.
Q2. Environmental Action Plan at a Local Heritage Site:

Suppose you visit a local temple during a festival and notice heaps of disposable plastic cups, plates, and wrappers thrown around. Write four steps your school's junior club can take.

Model Answer:
1. Organize a weekend "Clean Heritage Campaign" with gloves and trash bags.
2. Place separate labeled dustbins for degradable and non-degradable waste.
3. Make colorful placards with slogans like "Keep Our Holy Sites Clean".
4. Request temple priest and festival organizers to provide leaf plates (*Tapari*) instead of single-use plastic.
Q3. Slogan & Awareness Creation:
Write two catchy slogans:

• Slogan 2 (On Peaceful Dialogue): "Cool Minds Talk, Warm Hearts Win — Say No to Anger, Let Peace Begin!"
