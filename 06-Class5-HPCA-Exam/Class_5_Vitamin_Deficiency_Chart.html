🥗 Complete Vitamin & Nutrition Study Chart
Diseases Caused, Major Symptoms & Food Sources You Must Eat Every Day
👁 Vitamin A (Retinol)
Eye & Vision
⚠ Disease Caused: Night Blindness (रतोन्धो)
🔍 Symptoms: Cannot see properly in dim light or after sunset, severe dryness of the eyes, dry and rough skin.
🥕 How to Get It: Carrots, ripe papaya, ripe mango, pumpkin, spinach (palungo), egg yolk, milk, butter, fish oil.
⚡ Vitamin B-Complex (B1, B2, B12)
Energy & Nerves
⚠ Disease Caused: Beriberi • Mouth Sores
🔍 Symptoms: Extreme tiredness, weak muscles, loss of appetite, swollen feet, cracks at the corners of the lips.
🍚 How to Get It: Whole wheat roti, brown rice, pulses/daal, peanuts, green peas, eggs, bananas, dairy milk.
🍊 Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)
Gums & Immunity
⚠ Disease Caused: Scurvy (स्कर्भी)
🔍 Symptoms: Bleeding and swollen gums, loose teeth, cuts take a very long time to heal, frequent colds and infections.
🍋 How to Get It: Amla (super-rich!), oranges, lemons, sweet lime, guava (amba), tomatoes, green chilies, strawberries.
☀️ Vitamin D (Sunshine Vitamin)
Bones & Teeth
⚠ Disease Caused: Rickets (रिकेट्स)
🔍 Symptoms: Soft and weak bones, bent legs (bow-legs or knock-knees), delayed growth of teeth, weak spine.
☀️ How to Get It: 15-20 mins in morning sunlight, cow's milk, cheese, curd, egg yolks, fish, fortified butter.
🤝 Vitamin E (Tocopherol)
Skin & Cell Shield
⚠ Disease Caused: Muscle Weakness & Poor Skin
🔍 Symptoms: Lack of muscle strength, walking clumsiness, dull dry skin, low resistance to sickness.
🥜 How to Get It: Almonds, peanuts, sunflower seeds, vegetable oils (mustard/sunflower), green leafy spinach, whole grain cereals.
🩸 Vitamin K (Phylloquinone)
Blood Clotting
⚠ Disease Caused: Excessive Bleeding (Hemorrhage)
🔍 Symptoms: Blood does not clot quickly after minor cuts, frequent nosebleeds, easy dark bruises under the skin.
🥦 How to Get It: Cabbage (banda), cauliflower, broccoli, spinach, lettuce, green beans, soybean, green peas.
💎 Also in Class 5 Syllabus: 3 Essential Body Minerals
Mineral Disease Caused Symptoms to Look For How to Get It (Foods)
Iron (फलाम) Anaemia (रक्तअल्पता) Pale skin and pale fingernails, tiredness, dizziness, breathlessness when playing. Spinach (palungo), apples, pomegranate, lentils/beans, jaggery (gund), green veg.
Iodine (आयोडिन) Goitre (गण्डमाला) Swelling in front of the neck (thyroid gland), slow physical and mental growth in children. Iodized table salt (two-child logo salt), seafood, sea fish.
Calcium (क्याल्सियम) Weak Bones & Tooth Decay Fragile brittle bones, painful muscle cramps, poor enamel, teeth cavity. Milk, yogurt/curd, cheese, sesame seeds (til), ragi/finger millet (kodo).
