Chapter 1 • Ruskin Bond

Grandpa Tickles a Tiger

Grandfather finds a shivering tiger cub named Timothy in the Dehra forest. Raised with puppy Toto, Timothy eventually grows wild and is sent to Lucknow Zoo. Months later, Grandfather visits and lovingly pets a tiger in the cage, smacking its jaw when it growls. The keeper rushes over in horror: Timothy had died of pneumonia two months ago—this was a fierce wild tiger trapped last month!

Q: Why did Grandfather smack the tiger?
It was his old gentle trick to keep Timothy quiet when he growled.

Q: What is the twist in the ending?
Grandfather risked his life without realizing it, yet kindness calmed a dangerous wild predator.
Chapter 2 • Folktale

A Giant and His Magic Goat

A poor, honest boy encounters a towering giant. Instead of terror, the boy shows respect and humility. In return, the giant rewards him with a magic goat that produces gold coins when spoken to truthfully. A greedy innkeeper swaps the goat for a normal one. The boy returns to the giant, who gives him an enchanted staff to retrieve the stolen goat and punish greed.

Core Theme: Honesty, courage, and true justice triumph over deception.

Rule for the Magic Goat: It must never be struck or lied to, representing respect for blessings.
Chapter 3 • Mystery & Suspense

The Locked Room

At the far end of an old country house sits a heavy oak door that has been locked for generations. Curious children find an antique brass key in a secret desk. Whispers and creaking floorboards build intense suspense. When the door finally opens, they do not find monsters or cobwebs—but their great-grandfather's breathtaking workshop of clockwork birds, miniature trains, and musical inventions!

Atmosphere Words: Creak, shadows, brass key, suspense, antique, mechanical.

Key Takeaway: Curiosity accompanied by respect turns irrational fear into wonder and discovery.

Reported Speech Transformation Rules • Class 5 Direct to Indirect ✓ 4 Cardinal Rules
1. Tense Shift (Backshift)
• Simple Present → Simple Past (play → played)
• Present Continuous → Past Continuous (is walking → was walking)
• Simple Past → Past Perfect (saw → had seen)
• Will / Can → Would / Could
2. Pronoun Adjustment
Change pronouns according to the speaker and listener:
• "I" → he / she
• "we" → they
• "my" → his / her
• "you" → I / they / him / her
3. Time & Place Shifts
• Now → Then
• Today → That day
• Yesterday → The previous day / day before
• Tomorrow → The next / following day
• Here → There | This → That
4. Questions & Commands
• Yes/No Questions use if or whether.
• Remove question mark; convert to statement order.
• Commands use ordered to / told to / warned not to.

Section A: Reading Comprehension (8 Marks)

Extract: Grandfather stroking and tickling the tiger in Lucknow Zoo until the keeper interrupts in panic...

• Actions: Put arms through railings, stroked forehead, tickled ears, playfully smacked mouth.
• Why tiger sprang back: Snarling leopard in adjacent cage.
• Zookeeper revelation: Timothy died of pneumonia 2 months ago; this tiger is a dangerous wild beast caught in the hills last month.
• Vocabulary: Relish (great enjoyment), Adjacent (next to).
• Antonym of fierce: Gentle / mild / tame.

Section B: Oxford Literature (16 Marks)

Questions from Grandpa Tickles a Tiger, A Giant and His Magic Goat, and The Locked Room.

• Q2 (Context): Grandpa said goodbye thinking it was Timothy. Giant gave the goat that produces wealth for honest truth.
• Q3 (Vocab): Creak → high-pitched door squeak | Pneumonia → lung illness | Stalk → pursue stealthily | Suspense → tense excitement/fear.
• Q4 (Short Answers): Timothy brought from Terai jungle, sent to zoo because he grew aggressive; Innkeeper swapped goat, boy retrieved it with magic stick; Locked room contained grandfather's clockwork inventions.

Section C: Grammar • Forms of Verbs & Reported Speech (18 Marks)

Conjugations, tense blanks, direct-to-indirect transformations, and error spotting.

• Verb Table: grow → grew; hide → hidden; speak → speaking; catch → caught; choose → chooses.
• Blanks: (a) known (b) brought (c) to creak / creaking (d) found.
• Reported Speech:
1. Grandfather said that he knew that tiger very well.
2. The keeper told Grandfather that his Timothy had died two months before.
3. The giant warned him not to betray his trust.
4. Mother asked Rohan if he had seen anyone unlock that room.
• Errors: 'is reading... yesterday' → 'was reading... the previous day'; 'told to' → 'told'; 'that if' → 'if'.
