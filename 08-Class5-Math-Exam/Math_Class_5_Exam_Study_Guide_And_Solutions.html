Crucial Calculation Rules:
• Conversion: Improper to Mixed = Quotient RemainderDivisor. Mixed to Improper = Whole × Den + NumDen.
• Lowest Terms (Simplest): Divide both top and bottom by their HCF until no common factor remains except 1.
• Addition / Subtraction: Find LCM of denominators to make them like fractions, then add/subtract numerators only!
• Multiplication: Product of NumeratorsProduct of Denominators (Cross-cancel common factors first!).
• Division (KCF Rule): Keep 1st fraction → Change ÷ to × → Flip 2nd fraction (Reciprocal).

Concept Definition Examples
Variable Letter/symbol representing unknown quantity x, y, a, m
Constant Fixed numerical value that does not change 5, −12, 100
Term Product of constant and variables In −7x²: Coeff=−7, Var=x
Like Terms Terms with exact same variables and powers 3xy and −8yx (Order doesn't matter)
Polynomials Monomial (1 term), Binomial (2), Trinomial (3) Binomial: 3x + 5
Algebraic Balancing Rules (Equations):
• Golden Scale Rule: Whatever you do to Left Hand Side (LHS), do to Right Hand Side (RHS)!
• Transposition Rules:
   • x + a = b &implies; x = b − a  (Addition becomes Subtraction)
   • x − a = b &implies; x = b + a  (Subtraction becomes Addition)
   • a · x = b &implies; x = b ÷ a  (Multiplication becomes Division)
   • xa = b &implies; x = b × a  (Division becomes Multiplication)

Q7. Multiplication & Division of Fractions 4 Marks
(a) 1516 × 825:
• Cross-cancel 8 and 16 by 8: 8÷8=1, 16÷8=2.
• Cross-cancel 15 and 25 by 5: 15÷5=3, 25÷5=5.
• Multiply remaining: 3 × 12 × 5 = 310.
Answer: 310
(b) 334 ÷ 58:
• Convert mixed number: 334 = 3×4 + 34 = 154.
• Apply KCF (Multiply by reciprocal of 58 which is 85):
  154 × 85
• Cross-cancel: 15 and 5 by 5 → 3 and 1; 8 and 4 by 4 → 2 and 1.
• Result = 3 × 21 × 1 = 6.
Answer: 6 (Whole Number)
Q8. Algebraic Expressions: Combining Like Terms & Subtraction 4 Marks
(a) Simplify: 7x + 5y − 3x + 4y + 8
• Step 1: Group like terms together:
  = (7x − 3x) + (5y + 4y) + 8
• Step 2: Combine coefficients:
  = (7 − 3)x + (5 + 4)y + 8
  = 4x + 9y + 8.
Answer: 4x + 9y + 8
(b) Subtract (3a − 4b) from (8a + 2b):
• Step 1: Write expression with brackets:
  (8a + 2b) − (3a − 4b)
• Step 2: Remove brackets (distribute minus sign):
  = 8a + 2b − 3a + 4b   (− × − becomes +!)
• Step 3: Combine like terms:
  = (8a − 3a) + (2b + 4b) = 5a + 6b.
Answer: 5a + 6b

Q9. Substitution and Evaluation 5 Marks
(a) Evaluate 3p² − 2q + 7 when p = 4, q = 3:
• Substitute values: 3 × (4)² − 2 × (3) + 7
• Powers first: (4)² = 16.
• Multiply: 3 × 16 = 48   |   2 × 3 = 6.
• Calculate: 48 − 6 + 7 = 42 + 7 = 49.
Answer: 49
(b) Evaluate 4x + 6y2z when x = 5, y = 2, z = 1:
• Numerator: 4(5) + 6(2) = 20 + 12 = 32.
• Denominator: 2(1) = 2.
• Divide: 322 = 16.
Answer: 16
Q10. Solving Linear Equations 5 Marks
(a) Solve & Verify: 4x − 7 = 17:
• Transpose −7 to RHS: 4x = 17 + 7 = 24.
• Transpose 4 to RHS: x = 244 = 6.
• Verification check:
  LHS = 4(6) − 7 = 24 − 7 = 17 = RHS (Verified ✓).
Answer: x = 6
(b) Solve: 3m + 52 = 10:
• Multiply both sides by 2: 3m + 5 = 10 × 2 = 20.
• Transpose +5 to RHS: 3m = 20 − 5 = 15.
• Divide by 3: m = 153 = 5.
Answer: m = 5
Q11. Word Problem: Age Problem in Algebra 4 Marks
Question Summary: Mother is 3 times as old as son. Sum of ages = 48.
• Step 1: Let the unknown variable:
  Let son's present age = x years.
  Then mother's age = 3 × x = 3x years.
• Step 2: Form equation from condition:
  Son's age + Mother's age = 48
  x + 3x = 48
• Step 3: Solve for x:
  4x = 48 &implies; x = 484 = 12.
• Step 4: Find both ages:
  Son's age = x = 12 years.
  Mother's age = 3x = 3 × 12 = 36 years.
• Check: 12 + 36 = 48 ✓.
Answer: Son = 12 years, Mother = 36 years
Q12. Real-Life Problem: Tokyo Pocket Money Fractions 4 Marks
Total Money: ¥6,000. Spent 25 on books & 13 on travel/manga.
• (i) Total fraction spent:
  = 25 + 13 (LCM of 5 and 3 = 15)
  = 2 × 315 + 1 × 515 = 6 + 515 = 1115.
(i) Fraction spent = 1115

• (ii) Fraction left with him:
  Total allowance is 1 whole (1515).
  Remaining = 1 − 1115 = 15 − 1115 = 415.
(ii) Fraction left = 415

• (iii) Actual money saved in Yen:
  Amount saved = 415 of ¥6,000
  = 415 × 6,000 = 4 × (6,000 ÷ 15) = 4 × 400 = ¥1,600.
(iii) Money Saved = ¥1,600

★ Parent & Tutor Teaching Master Tips (Class 5 Math in Japan) Teacher's Note

• Top Mistake in Fractions: Students often add numerators and denominators straight across (e.g. thinking 12 + 13 = 25). Remind them: "You cannot add slices of pizza unless they are the same size! LCM makes the slices equal first."
• Division of Fractions: Remind them the catchy phrase: "Keep the first, change to multiply, flip the second upside down!"
• Top Mistake in Algebra: In subtraction, students forget to change the sign of the second term: e.g. −(3a − 4b) becomes −3a + 4b.
• Linear Equations: Teach the "Bridge Rule" across the equal sign (=): "Whenever a number jumps over the bridge (=), it changes its outfit (sign): + becomes −, × becomes ÷."
